## Radius

```text
all component geometry  0px
```

The default product language is deliberately square. Controls, status elements, surfaces, and overlays do not introduce rounded variants.

## Borders

Thin borders and dividers are the primary structural device. Stronger borders may communicate selection, emphasis, or containment.

## Shadows

Use shadow for actual elevation:

- dropdowns
- popovers
- dialogs
- sticky surfaces that overlap content

Normal sections, tables, and cards should not float by default.
