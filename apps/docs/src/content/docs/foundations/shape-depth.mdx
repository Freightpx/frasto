---
title: Shape & depth
description: Restrained radius, quiet borders, and elevation only where separation is required.
---

## Radius

```text
small   4px
medium  8px
large   12px
```

The default product language is precise rather than bubbly. Avoid oversized rounding on every container.

## Borders

Thin borders and dividers are the primary structural device. Stronger borders may communicate selection, emphasis, or containment.

## Shadows

Use shadow for actual elevation:

- dropdowns
- popovers
- dialogs
- sticky surfaces that overlap content

Normal sections, tables, and cards should not float by default.
